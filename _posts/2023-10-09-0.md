# Course Requirements

Welcome to this course! To successfully complete the course and earn the credited CP, certain requirements must be fulfilled, as outlined below.

## 1. Attendance

Throughout the semester, you are permitted to miss a maximum of two (2) sessions. Attendance is generally mandatory. If you are unable to attend a class, please notify us in advance by sending an email to n.gibson@em.uni-frankfurt.de.

## 2. Assignments to be Completed

Each week, there will typically be an assigned reading that will be announced, and you are expected to prepare accordingly. Additionally, students requiring a "Leistungsschein" will need to either give a presentation or write an essay. For those who need to take an exam for this course, please consult the Study Regulations of your program or check the information available on OLAT. Make sure to inform me in advance about the type of exam you require.

## 3. General Informations

Our final session will take place on (date). If you have any questions about the course, feel free to email me at n.gibson@em.uni-frankfurt.de. My office hours are on (date). If you would like to have a one-on-one discussion, please email me in advance to arrange a meeting. If you decide not to continue with this course, kindly notify me as well.
